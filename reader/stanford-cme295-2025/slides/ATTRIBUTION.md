# Stanford CME 295 slide page sources

These WebP files are faithful, uncropped page renderings of the nine official Stanford CME 295 Autumn 2025 lecture PDFs. Page credits and marks remain visible in the rendered images.

- Official 2025 syllabus: [https://cme295.stanford.edu/syllabus/2025/](https://cme295.stanford.edu/syllabus/2025/)
- Official lecture playlist: [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOCXd21gf0CF4xr35yINeOy](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoROMvodv4rOCXd21gf0CF4xr35yINeOy)
- Per-deck PDF URLs, page counts, source hashes, and image hashes: [slides-index.json](../slides-index.json)
